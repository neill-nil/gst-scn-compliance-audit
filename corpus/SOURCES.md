The CBIC rules and forms PDFs are convenience consolidations; the PDF itself
warns that notified amendments have legal force. The forms PDF contains
DRC-01, DRC-01A, DRC-06 and DRC-07. Later amendments must be checked before
the forms or rules are treated as current. The three notifications above
modify section 73 order deadlines for specified years; their effect on a
particular notice still requires reading the full applicable sequence.
The 2021 instruction is useful for identifying due-date notifications, but
the notifications themselves must be cited for calculator inputs. The 2024
circular is relevant to jurisdiction and adjudicating authority; it does not
set a limitation date.
