CASE 16 — KAHC010289442026
Karnataka High Court, Bengaluru. WP No. 13471/2026 (T-RES).
M/S Shivneel Gas Sales vs Assistant Commissioner of Commercial Taxes (Audit) & Ors.
Decided (oral order): 30-09-2026. Single judge (B M Shyam Prasad, J.).
rules flag in CSV: "limitation"

FACTS STATED
- Provision: Section 73, KGST/CGST Act, 2017. Tax period: July 2017-March 2018 (FY 2017-18).
- SCN (Form GST DRC-01, "Summary of the Show Cause Notice") dated 30-09-2023,
  file no. T.No.294/2023-24, SCN no. ACCT/ENF/CKM/ADT/2023-24.
- Adjudication order dated 01-12-2023 under s.73(9); Summary of the Order
  (DRC-07, ref ZD291223004521V) dated 02-12-2023.
- No hearing date, service mode, or signature fact is stated or discussed anywhere
  in the order — those are simply not in issue in this case.
- Petitioner had filed GST returns for Aug 2017-Apr 2019 late (between Apr-Aug 2019).

DEFECTS ARGUED
- None of the contracts.md defect types. The sole ground actually argued and
  decided is substantive, not procedural: that the retrospective insertion of
  s.16(5) CGST Act (w.e.f. 01-07-2017, allowing ITC on returns filed up to
  30-11-2021 for FY2017-18 to FY2020-21) entitles the petitioner to ITC that
  the 01-12-2023 order denied for late-filed returns.
- The prayer clause also sought declarations that s.16(4) CGST Act is
  unconstitutional and that the s.168A extension notifications
  (No.9/2023-CT dated 31-03-2023 and the parallel KGST notification
  06/2023 dated 06-04-2023) are ultra vires — but the court never reaches
  these; it disposes of the petition on the narrower s.16(5) point alone.

COURT'S DECISION
- Petition allowed. The 01-12-2023 adjudication order is quashed and the
  matter is restored to the officer to pass a fresh/formal order applying
  s.16(5)'s retrospective ITC entitlement, after giving the petitioner an
  opportunity. Officer to communicate the fresh order by 30-10-2026;
  petitioner to be informed by 30-11-2026.
- No finding, rejection, or "not decided" on any schema defect type —
  there is nothing to score for RQ1 here.

SCHEMA/PIPELINE NOTE
- The rule-based "limitation" flag on this case looks like a false positive:
  the judgment never discusses limitation at all (not SCN timing, not the
  s.168A extension notifications' validity in substance — those are prayed
  for but not adjudicated). Worth checking against b0_rules.py once written;
  this case is a useful "no relevant defect in scope" / clean negative
  example for the eval harness, and a caution that keyword rules can
  mis-flag a case whose real issue is unrelated (here, s.16(5) ITC).

CASE 17 — UPHC010748612025
Allahabad High Court (Chief Justice's Court, Division Bench). WRIT TAX No. 526/2025.
Rajesh Kumar Singh vs Commissioner of Commercial Tax, UP Lucknow & Anr.
Order date: 19-02-2025. Bench: Arun Bhansali, CJ and Kshitij Shailendra, J.
rules flag in CSV: "hearing_75_4;non_speaking"

FACTS STATED
- Tax period: April 2018-March 2019 (GTA/transport business, ITC dispute).
- Two separate earlier-stage proceedings mentioned: (1) a Section 61 scrutiny
  notice (discrepancies after return scrutiny), reply due 22-02-2023, "personal
  hearing in this regard was denied in the notice" — this is pre-SCN scrutiny,
  not the s.73 notice itself.
- SCN issued under Section 73(1) on 23-12-2023: reply due date AND personal
  hearing date were both fixed for the same day, 20-01-2024.
- Petitioner filed reply on 18-01-2024 (10 points; RCM-based defence — tax on
  the transactions is payable by the recipient under reverse charge).
- Adjudication order dated 30-04-2024 under Section 73(9): accepted 7 of the
  10 points without giving reasons; on the other 3, recorded only a cursory
  Hindi finding that no RCM-tax-deposit evidence/account verification was
  produced — did not engage with the RCM submission specifically.
- Petitioner then filed a Section 161 rectification application (same RCM
  ground); rejected by order dated 09-10-2024.

DEFECTS ARGUED
- hearing_75_4: no real opportunity of personal hearing — identical dates
  for reply and hearing made the hearing an "empty formality," violating
  natural justice (also framed as a s.75(4) violation).
- non_speaking_order: the 30-04-2024 order is "absolutely non speaking,"
  ignores the specific RCM evidence/documents filed with the reply.
- (also challenged the s.161 rectification rejection of 09-10-2024, but only
  as a consequence of the main order's invalidity — not an independent
  schema defect type.)

COURT'S DECISION
- hearing_75_4: defect_found. Court states it has "repeatedly frowned on"
  the practice of fixing an identical reply-due date and hearing date,
  calling it an empty formality that results in violation of natural justice.
- non_speaking_order: defect_found. Court quotes the vernacular finding
  verbatim and holds a "cursory disposal" of a "very specific" plea cannot
  be sustained.
- Both the 30-04-2024 adjudication order and the 09-10-2024 rectification
  order under s.161 are quashed (the latter "loses its significance" once
  the former falls). Petition allowed; matter remanded to the Deputy
  Commissioner, State Tax, Mirzapur to decide afresh after a proper hearing.

SCHEMA/PIPELINE NOTE
- Two notices appear in the facts (s.61 scrutiny notice, then the s.73 SCN).
  Only the s.73 SCN/order is the in-scope "notice" object; the s.61 scrutiny
  stage is background only — worth a guideline example of what to exclude
  from `notice`/`events`.

CASE 18 — UPHC020953002025
Allahabad High Court, Lucknow Bench. WRIT TAX No. 1479/2025.
M/S Agarwal and Company (Prop. Anubhav Agarwal) vs State of U.P. & Ors.
Decided: 04-12-2025. Jaspreet Singh, J.
rules flag in CSV: "hearing_75_4;beyond_scn_75_7"

FACTS STATED
- Order under Section 73 dated 29-04-2024; a first appeal against it was
  dismissed as time-barred by order dated 01-04-2025.
- Respondent's counsel admits "no date was fixed for personal hearing" in
  the proceeding.
- The impugned order records the petitioner appearing/replying on three
  dates — 23-09-2022, 07-10-2022, 27-10-2022 — in response to earlier
  notices; after the 27-10-2022 reply, the adjudicating authority decided
  the matter on merits without issuing any further notice or offering an
  oral personal hearing.
- Court relies heavily on and reproduces paras 5-11 of a Division Bench
  judgment, Mahaveer Trading Company vs Deputy Commissioner (W.T. No.303/2024,
  2024:AHC:38820-DB), including a UP Commercial Tax Dept. Office Memo
  No.1406 dated 12-11-2024 listing systemic defects: hearing date marked
  "N.A.", hearing date fixed before the reply-due date, hearing date same
  as reply-due date, and order date not matching the hearing date.

DEFECTS ARGUED
- hearing_75_4: no opportunity of personal hearing before the adverse order.
- Petitioner's counsel also asserted the order is "violative of Section
  75(7)" (i.e., a demand_beyond_scn-type point) but the judgment does not
  develop or separately reason through this ground anywhere in the text.
- The 01-04-2025 appellate dismissal (limitation) is quashed too, but only
  as a consequence of the main order falling — it is not itself argued as
  a `limitation` defect of the SCN/order.

COURT'S DECISION
- hearing_75_4: defect_found, by applying Mahaveer Trading Company's ratio
  to this case's own facts (no further notice/hearing was offered before
  the merits order) — a clean example of the "relying on another judgment"
  rule in role4_schema_eval.md: it counts as a finding here because the
  court applies the precedent's reasoning to this case's specific facts,
  not merely quoting it.
- s.75(7)/demand-beyond-scn point: not decided — mentioned by counsel but
  never engaged with independently in the court's reasoning.
- Both the 29-04-2024 order and the 01-04-2025 appellate order are quashed;
  petition allowed; remanded to the assessing authority for a fresh order
  after giving a hearing.

SCHEMA/PIPELINE NOTE
- Good guideline example for "court relying on another judgment ('covered
  by') → a finding only if the court applies it to this case's facts" —
  here it clearly does apply it, so defect_found is correct, not a quote-only
  case.
- The s.75(7) ground that is argued but never actually reasoned through is a
  good "not_decided" guideline example (distinct from a consent/remand
  not_decided — here it is simply left unaddressed).

CASE 19 — WBCHCJ0026132026
Calcutta High Court, Circuit Bench at Jalpaiguri. WPA 991 of 2026.
M/s Garg Enterprises vs Additional Commissioner, CGST & CX, Siliguri Commissionerate & Anr.
Decided: 02-09-2026. Raja Basu Chowdhury, J.
rules flag in CSV: "hearing_75_4;signature;summary_only_142;non_speaking"

FACTS STATED
- Order under Section 74, CGST/WBGST Act, for tax period January 2020 to
  November 2023 (one SCN spanning multiple financial years).
- Pre-SCN in Form DRC-1A — petitioner alleges it carries no/invalid
  signature of the proper officer.
- Summary of SCN (Form DRC-01) uploaded on the portal, dated 14-08-2025;
  detailed SCN reasons given in a composite document covering all periods
  together (investigation into alleged smuggling of cement from Bhutan and
  concealment of GST liability on its sale).
- Impugned order dated 27-03-2026.
- Procedural history: petitioner earlier filed WPA 11/2026 against the same
  SCN, then withdrew it with liberty (order of a co-ordinate bench,
  17-03-2026) to file a belated reply and be heard; the proper officer was
  given liberty to decide on merits — no liberty was reserved to come back
  to the High Court on the same cause of action.
- Petitioner did file a reply and was heard (represented by advocate), but
  the hearing was conducted by a different officer than the one with whom
  the written response was filed — though both are found to be "proper
  officer" of the same office.

DEFECTS ARGUED (petition lists five grounds, para 3(a)-(e))
(a) signature — pre-SCN (DRC-1A) not signed / invalid signature.
(b) reply_not_considered — detailed SCN (DRC-01) issued without considering
    the response already filed to the pre-SCN.
(c) summary_only / vagueness — SCN vague and non-specific, no taxable event
    or essential s.74 ingredients disclosed (closest schema fit:
    section_74_ingredients / other_procedural; doesn't map cleanly to one type).
(d) consolidated_scn — SCN clubs different tax periods/financial years
    together, said to be impermissible.
(e) jurisdiction/hearing irregularity — reply was filed with one authority
    (Addl. Commissioner, Siliguri) but the hearing was conducted, and order
    passed, by a different authority.
- An implicit non_speaking_order argument also runs through: that the order
  was passed with a "pre-determined notion" and ignored the objections raised.

COURT'S DECISION (goes through each ground)
- (a) signature: not decided on merits — court finds the detailed SCN is on
  file and (on this record, without the underlying file being produced)
  declines to adjudicate the disputed factual question of whether the
  pre-SCN's signature is invalid; calls it a disputed factual issue for the
  statutory forum, not the writ court.
- (b) reply not considered on pre-SCN: not separately addressed — folded
  into the general "disputed facts" finding above.
- (c) vagueness / (d) consolidated_scn: defect_rejected on the merits — the
  court finds the DRC-01 summary (dated 14-08-2025) clearly identifies the
  demand and the specific taxable period, with no clubbing in the summary
  itself; the composite detailed reasoning across periods is justified
  because a single connected investigation (cement smuggling) covers all
  of them.
- non_speaking_order: defect_rejected — court holds "prima facie sufficient
  reason" was given and the order "cannot be said to be non-speaking,"
  though again flags this as touching disputed facts.
- (e) different-authority/jurisdiction point: defect_rejected — same
  "proper officer" confirmed to have received the reply and heard/decided
  the matter; petitioner was heard and represented by counsel.
- Overall disposition: writ petition DISMISSED — not on any of the above
  findings alone, but because an effective statutory appellate remedy
  exists (with a mandatory pre-deposit) and the petitioner expressly
  refused to make that pre-deposit; the remaining disputed factual issues
  (signature, etc.) are left to that forum. Liberty reserved to appeal.

SCHEMA/PIPELINE NOTE
- Useful example of mixed findings within one case: some grounds rejected
  on the merits (vagueness/consolidated_scn, non_speaking, different-
  authority), one left genuinely undecided as a disputed fact (signature),
  and the petition as a whole dismissed on an alternate-remedy/laches basis
  rather than on the merits of any defect — a good guideline case for
  distinguishing per-ground defect_rejected/not_decided from the case-level
  `outcome.disposition` (here: dismissed, not remanded).
- `consolidated_scn` here spans financial years AND a multi-year "tax
  period" (Jan 2020-Nov 2023) rather than cleanly separated FYs — worth
  checking the `proceeding.financial_years` field can represent a
  continuous cross-FY span, not just a list of whole FYs.
